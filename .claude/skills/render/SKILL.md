# Render — music, sound, captions, final export

Input: `videos/<video>/` with an approved `storyboard.json` (fitted to the voice: has `audio`
and `timing`) and an approved `audio/voice-script.json`. Output:

```
audio/music.wav        chosen or generated music
audio/mix.wav          final soundtrack (voice + ducked music + SFX), -14 LUFS
audio/mix.json         every sound event (shot, cue, file, time) for checking
render/final.mp4       1080x1920 (per format), 30 fps, H.264 + AAC
render/cover.png       full-resolution cover / thumbnail
render/qc.json         quality-check report
```

This stage **doesn't change visuals or words.** Wrong animation or timing → storyboard /
voiceover skills. **Free by default:** library or code-generated music, code-generated
sound effects. Paid music (AI generation) only if the user asks or approves an estimate.

## Step 0 — One video from several parts (pre-hook)

A pre-hook / cold open built as its own small video in `<video>/prehook/` (its own script, voice and fit) is
joined to the main board **before** this stage: `node studio/scripts/master.mjs videos/<video>` → `<video>/master/`
(one storyboard, one voice track, one timings file; music / captions / sfxDb settings kept on rebuild). Run
Steps 1–5 on `videos/<video>/master`, then copy `master/render/final.mp4`, `cover.png`, `qc.json` to
`<video>/render/`. After any part changes: re-fit that part, rebuild the master, re-mix, re-render.
Compute music times (the lift, `--silence`) on the master's timeline (part 1's length shifts everything).

## Step 1 — Music

1. Read the storyboard's `music.mood` / `notes` and the video length.
2. Look in `studio/library/music/` (`index.json` lists tracks with mood tags, bpm, licence).
   Pick the best match; if none fits, say so.
3. No match → generate the free fallback, with the lift ("drop") on the reveal shot's start
   (scene start time — compute from durations/transitions):
   `node studio/scripts/make-music.mjs --out videos/<video>/audio/music.wav --duration <s> --mood upbeat|calm|cinematic|tense --bpm <n> --drop <s> [--silence <from>-<to>]`
   (`tense`: minor key, heartbeat pulse, unresolved ending; `--silence` mutes a window before a
   reveal sound — in tense mood a ticking clock follows it). Universe video: follow
   `universe.json` → `music` / `sfx`.
   Library tracks: copy/convert to `audio/music.wav`; trimming, fading and ducking happen in the mix.
4. Record it in `storyboard.json` → `music.file`, `music.source` (track + licence, or
   "code-generated"), optional `music.db` (default -16 dB before ducking).

## Step 2 — Sound effects

Cues already live in each shot's `sfx` (plain words, anchored to frames or spoken words by
the voiceover stage). The mixer maps words → sounds via `studio/library/sfx/index.json`
(generated by `node studio/scripts/make-sfx.mjs`; custom sounds go in `custom`, checked first).
`repeat` + `every` play a cue several times; `syncEnd: true` places it at the shot's end;
risers end on their cue. Transition whooshes are opt-in (`--auto-whoosh`) — the user
prefers restrained sound: signature moments only (≈ 1 cue per 2 s at most, none on most
transitions). If a storyboard has far more cues than that, thin them to the key moments
before mixing and say so. Fix any "No sound matched" lines from the mixer by adding
keywords or a custom sound — never by changing the storyboard's design.

Keywords match at the start of a word ("ping" no longer catches "scraping"); still read `audio/mix.json` once
and check each cue's file fits what is on screen: a "door" cue must not land on the knock when the shot is a
cell door or cupboard (use "cell door" → metal-clang, "thud" → punch). Library additions 2026-10-16:
coin-jingle, metal-clang, soil-scrape; custom keywords rustle → card-flick, notification → chime,
shutter / pin into → pop, thud → punch. A ticking bed comes from the tense music after `--silence` — don't add a
"clock tick" cue (it maps to a pen tick). New sounds go in `make-sfx.mjs` (deterministic; re-running leaves the
existing files byte-identical — check with `cmp`) or as files in `custom`.

## Step 3 — Mix

`node studio/scripts/mix.mjs videos/<video>` (`--dry` to preview the event count first)

Voice stays on top; music is ducked by the voice (sidechain) and fades at the end; SFX at
-12 dB (`--sfx-db`, or `storyboard.sfxDb`); master limited and normalised to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP. Sets
`storyboard.finalAudio`. Re-run after any audio change.

## Step 4 — Captions

Default on for social formats. In `storyboard.json`:
`"captions": { "enabled": true, "y": 83, "size": 50, "skipLines": ["L11"] }`
- Phrases (≤ 4 words, balanced, split on pauses) come from the voice timings; the spoken
  word is highlighted in the brand's highlight colour.
- `skipLines`: VO lines whose words are already on screen (phone numbers, URLs).
- Per shot: `captionY` (% of frame) to move them, `captions: false` to hide them where the
  same words are on screen (e.g. a name lower third).
- Keep them inside the safe zone (default 83%) and off important design elements.

Check with one contact sheet (captions show on frames where someone speaks):
`node studio/scripts/render.mjs videos/<video>/storyboard.json --sheet --scale 0.25 --out <scratchpad>/captions-sheet.png`
Look once; fix collisions with `captionY` / `captions: false`.

## Step 5 — Final render, cover, QC

```
node studio/scripts/render.mjs videos/<video>/storyboard.json --final
node studio/scripts/render.mjs videos/<video>/storyboard.json --cover
node studio/scripts/qc.mjs videos/<video>
```

QC must pass: resolution, frame rate, codecs, length = storyboard, loudness -14 ±1.5 LUFS.
Warnings (true peak, silent gaps, black frames) → explain or fix. Don't watch the MP4;
send `render/final.mp4` (and the cover) to the user with the QC summary and what's in the
mix (music source, number of sound events, captions on/off).

## Step 6 — Approve

The final render of a ~100 s video takes several minutes: run final + cover + QC as one background command.

User happy → `"status": "final"` in `storyboard.json` → `render`, done. Changes:
music/sound/caption tweaks → Steps 1–5 here; visual or timing changes → storyboard /
voiceover skill, then re-run this stage.
