# Direction — Promo / ad

Goal on screen: **desire + a clear next step**. Energetic, confident, every second sells.

| | |
|-|-|
| Scene length | 1.5–3 s; never hold a static frame > 3.5 s without motion |
| Cuts | On the VO's sentence breaks; split long beats into 2–3 scenes |
| Transitions | Fast (8–10 frames): `pushCut`, `slide`, `wipe`. One `iris`/`flip` max as a "reveal" moment |
| Screen density | One idea per scene; max ~6 words on screen at once |
| Motion | Snappy or bouncy; numbers pop, buttons pulse |
| Music | Upbeat, driving; drops slightly under the problem beat, lifts at solution |
| SFX density | Medium: signature moments only — hit on the hook number, one sound on the reveal, a tick per benefit, chime on the CTA; ≈ 1 cue per 2 s at most, no whoosh on every transition |

## Beat → scene patterns

| Beat | Typical scenes | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Hook | NumberSlam (result/proof) · TextCard (pain/promise) | First frame must already show the hook text — no fade from black |
| Problem | TextCard with `emphasis` on the pain word | Darker surface (`secondary`) to contrast with the solution |
| Solution | LogoReveal → PersonSpotlight / OfferCards / StatCounter | Brand appears by ~30% of runtime; show the real people/products |
| Benefits | OfferCards (real products/services) · ListCards · TextCard per point | Max 3 points; each appears as the VO says it |
| Proof | Testimonial (photo + quote) · RatingStars · StatCounter | Real numbers/quotes only, exactly as in the script |
| Offer + CTA | EndCard | Hold ≥ 2 s after the VO ends; phone/link large |

## Avoid

- Brand colours fighting: one accent per scene
- Transitions longer than 12 frames (feels slow in an ad)
- Ending on anything other than the EndCard
