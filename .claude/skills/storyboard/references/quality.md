# Quality bar — creative, professional, clear

Run this on every scene and on the board as a whole before showing anything. A board that
fails any **must** line is not ready.

## Message first (must)

- One focal point per scene: the eye knows where to go in under 0.5 s.
- The on-screen words are verbatim from the script and readable at phone size:
  body ≥ 44 px, key text ≥ 72 px at 1080 wide; never more than ~8 words landing at once.
- Contrast: text on images sits on a panel, gradient or shadow; brand `usage` rules respected.
- Hold time ≥ reading time once the text has landed (~0.4 s per short line, 2.5 words/s for sentences).
- Social safe zones (9:16): keep key text and buttons out of the top 12% and bottom 20%
  (platform UI), and away from the right 10% (action buttons).
- The hook is readable on frame 0 with the sound off.

## Craft (must)

- ≥ 3 depth layers (background · mid · foreground) in every scene.
- Something moves in every frame: camera move, parallax, loop, particles or texture.
- Every scene has a camera decision (push, pull, pan, tilt, shake) — even a slow 3–8% push.
- Elements enter in sequence with intent (most important first), not all at once.
- Real assets wherever the library has them: people as cut-outs or framed, products as cards.
- Finish: grain and/or vignette on photographic or textured scenes.

## Characters in a place (must, illustrated scenes with characters)

- The character belongs to the light of the set: lit (tint + shading toward the key light),
  rim-lit by a motivated coloured source, and grounded by at least a contact shadow.
- Something overlaps the character's lower body or the frame edge (grass, fence, counter).
- The background breathes: 2–4 subtle ambient-life layers (fog, wind, flicker, stars…).
- Check it on the contact sheet: if a character reads as a sticker, the light is too weak or
  the set is missing its foreground.
- `studio/scripts/board-check.mjs` passes (no errors; warnings fixed or explained).
- Coverage: shot sizes and angles vary (no two consecutive shots alike); the character is not
  always facing front — profile, three-quarter and back views where the action motivates them;
  at least one walk or turn per location; screen direction is kept across cuts.

## Creativity (must)

- The concept's motif appears in most scenes (e.g. red-pen marks, paper, a shape, a colour).
- Transitions are motivated (match the motif, continue a movement, cut on an impact) —
  not the same wipe everywhere.
- Read the narration across each cut: the next shot continues the action, question
  or argument. Scene changes do not impose spoken labels or audio gaps; connected
  narration may continue through several scenes.
- At least 2 "signature moments" in the video (a slam with shake, a draw-on, a depth reveal).
- Colour script: the palette shifts with the story (e.g. dark tension → bright answer).

## Restraint (avoid)

- Effects that hide the message (heavy blur, busy particles behind text).
- More than one signature effect per scene.
- A physics pile or brush marks covering the scene's key text; physics in more than two shots.
- Text animating so fast it can't be read; letter-by-letter on long lines.
- Decoration in the safe-zone bands.
- Too many sound cues: signature moments only (≈ 1 per 2 s at most); not every pop,
  stroke or transition needs a sound.
