# Format — Day One (Noxverse)

A second-person consequence story: the viewer ("you") has just done something big and illegal or
reckless, and real, sourced facts make day one go wrong, step by step. First used in
`videos/2026-10-14-faked-death-day-one` ("You just faked your own death").

## For the write stage (words only)

- **Type:** storytelling (series cliffhanger). 45–60 s, ~2.6 wps, present tense, "you".
- **Hook pattern:** "You just <did X>. Day one goes worse than you think." (on screen: "DAY 1: …")
- **Spine:** the plan → the legal/real-world catch → 3 problems, each a sourced fact that takes
  something away → a cliffhanger moment → audience vote.
- **The turn:** a reversal line early ("<X> usually isn't a crime by itself. Almost everything after it is.").
- **Problems:** label them on screen ("PROBLEM 1 · THE BODY"), never in VO. Each problem changes the
  score (money, identity, traces …) — name the 2–4 score fields in the brief as required on-screen.
- **Facts:** verify and source every legal/real-world claim; hedge where law varies ("usually",
  "up to"). No how-to detail that would help someone actually do it; don't glamorise.
- **Ending:** cliffhanger (a knock, a call, a face on TV), then the vote: "<A>, or <B>? Comment one word.
  The majority writes part two." On screen: "<A> or <B>" | "The majority writes Part 2".
